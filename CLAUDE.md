# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## What This Project Is

MyHandyman is an AI-powered handyman and home repair app that helps everyday people diagnose household problems, understand what tools and materials they need, and get step-by-step repair instructions. The goal is to make home repair feel easier, faster, and less intimidating.

Work in this folder spans product strategy, UX and UI planning, feature specs, pricing and monetization, app messaging, onboarding flows, prompt engineering, launch strategy, growth ideas, and landing page copy. It may eventually include code and product assets.

MyHandyman should be treated as a consumer product first. It is not just an AI demo or generic utility. The experience should feel polished, trustworthy, useful, and premium enough that a normal consumer would feel comfortable relying on it in their home.

## Target Users

Homeowners, renters, DIY beginners, and everyday people who need help fixing things around the house. Some want quick repair help; others want guided, step-by-step instructions. They care about simplicity, trust, speed, clear instructions, and feeling confident they can complete a repair correctly.

## Business Goals

- Near-term: improve the product, create a premium user experience, launch successfully, and acquire paying users.
- Longer-term: grow into a scalable consumer brand with strong retention, word of mouth, and subscription or affiliate revenue.

## Product Priorities

When suggesting ideas or features, prioritize in this order:
1. User trust and confidence
2. Simplicity and ease of use
3. Clear repair guidance and usefulness
4. Conversion to paid usage or monetization
5. Retention, repeat usage, and word of mouth

Prefer practical features that make the app more valuable and easier to use over flashy features that add complexity.

## Brand and Tone

Modern, helpful, trustworthy, practical, polished, and easy to understand. Premium and smart, but not corporate or robotic. Friendly and capable — like a reliable expert helping you get things done.

## UX Standards

- The user experience should feel clean, modern, and easy to follow
- Instructions should be simple, direct, and confidence-building
- Avoid clutter, confusion, or too many choices at once
- Design should feel premium, not cheap or overly playful
- Whenever possible, reduce user uncertainty and help them feel guided step by step
- Prioritize mobile-friendly thinking, since many users may use the app while working on a repair

## Working Principles

- Prioritize user trust, product clarity, and ease of use above all else.
- Keep recommendations practical and focused on building a premium consumer product.
- Suggestions should improve conversion, retention, usability, or user confidence.
- Do not make the app feel cheap, generic, or cluttered.
- Do not overcomplicate the user experience.
- When uncertain, ask focused questions instead of making assumptions.
- Flag ideas that may hurt trust, create liability concerns, or make the app feel unreliable.
